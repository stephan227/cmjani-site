Privacy

This site uses no cookies and no analytics.

Who runs this site?

This site is run by Jani (CMJani). You can reach me through my YouTube channel.

What data does the site collect?

The site doesn't collect data about you itself. It has no cookies, analytics, ad tracking or forms. Fonts and images load from this site.

Who handles technical data?

The site runs on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare processes technical data, such as IP addresses, to deliver pages and keep them secure. Read more in Cloudflare's privacy policy.

What happens when I play a video?

The video only loads when you press play. Your browser then connects to YouTube, and your data is handled under Google's privacy policy.

What about links to other sites?

Links lead to services such as Google Maps and YouTube. Partners behind affiliate links may use cookies under their own policies. Those services are responsible for their own privacy practices.

What are your rights?

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data. You can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman in Finland.
