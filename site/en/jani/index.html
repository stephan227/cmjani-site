Who is Jani?

Southeast Asia's special correspondent, at your service with 20 years of experience.

Hi, I'm Jani, known on YouTube as CMJani, or Chiang Mai Jani. I'm a Finnish special correspondent for Southeast Asia, with 20 years of experience in the region.

I got to know Chiang Mai's mountain trails about twenty years ago, when old friends took me to the Monk's Trail. Hundreds of forest trips have followed. I started the CMJani channel in March 2026, and it now has more than 30 videos and 38,000 views.

What happens in my videos?

  • Motorbikes: the Mae Hong Son Loop in four parts, including the 762 bends to Pai.
  • Hiking: jungle trails and mountain tops around Chiang Mai.
  • Food: khao soi tests and the places people queue for every day.
  • Travel: Bangkok, Koh Sichang, Hanoi, Cat Ba, Da Nang and Hue.
  • Saunas: yes, including the best sauna in Pai and the world's strangest one.
