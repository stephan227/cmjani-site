Easy or insane? Hiking Monk's Trail and Doi Pui in Chiang Mai

A relaxed jungle walk on the Monk's Trail and a sweaty climb to a mountain viewpoint near Doi Pui. Every place from the video, my measurements and tips.

Play video

Published 26 October 2026, 27 min 44 s long. The video is in Finnish; English subtitles are available on YouTube.

In short

The Monk's Trail is an easy loop of about two hours to the Wat Pha Lat jungle temple. The Doi Pui route is challenging: I measured 9.65 km, 530 m of ascent and 2 h 55 min. Both routes are in Doi Suthep–Pui National Park.

Which route is easier?

The Monk's Trail is clearly easier. Doi Pui is for fitter hikers, and I'd suggest everyone else stick to smaller trips.

What did I measure on the Doi Pui route?

My app finished at 9.65 km, 530 m of climbing and 2 hours 55 minutes (25:46). It estimated about 1,400 calories burned. The estimate at the start was about 7 km, so the route ran well over.

It was only about 25–26 °C, but a sweatband was still essential (19:34). The climb from the village was hard on the legs, and the way back to the bikes ran through a humid swamp and dense jungle full of blackflies.

How much does the Monk's Trail cost?

The Monk's Trail costs 100 baht for foreign adults and 20 baht for Thai adults (as of October 2026). The national park started charging in October 2025, and the ticket point appears in the video at 9:37. Check the amount on site. Almost all other trails in the area are still free.

When is the best time to hike in Chiang Mai?

October to January is the best time. After the rains, nature is at its greenest and the air cools down. The smoke season starts in February, when breathing hard on the slopes is a bad idea. In May the smoke gives way to the hot season, and after that the rainy season makes the trails difficult until September.

How do you find the trails?

The free basic version of the AllTrails app is enough. It shows almost all the trails in the area, plus distance and elevation gain. If you'd rather not go alone, Chiang Mai has several hiking groups, and travel agencies run guided hikes.

Are there dangerous animals on the trails?

Rarely. In hundreds of forest trips I've seen snakes only a few times, the last being a large king cobra more than ten years ago. You'll mostly meet birds, butterflies, small lizards and spiders.

What should you know before you go?

Bring a sweatband and something against blackflies. Some national park trails can close during the rainy season, so check before you go.

What should you eat after a hike?

Soup. Straight after the route, the talk turned to food, and Chiang Mai's best-known soup is khao soi. See what I found when I tested three khao soi restaurants in Chiang Mai.

How do you fly from Finland to Chiang Mai?

Finnair flies nonstop from Helsinki to Bangkok, with a scheduled flight time of about 11 hours 15 minutes. From Bangkok you take a domestic flight to Chiang Mai, which typically takes about 1 hour 20 minutes. Chiang Mai airport is only 3 to 5 km from the Old City. You can get into town with the airport taxi desk, or with the Grab and Bolt apps.

Search flights from Helsinki (HEL) to Chiang Mai (CNX) on Google Flights. Prices and schedules change, so check them when you search.
