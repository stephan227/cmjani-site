Khao soi in Chiang Mai: three restaurants put to the test

What do people queue for every day in Chiang Mai? I tested three khao soi spots, with a gym, markets and live rock in between.

Play video

Published 3 October 2026, 28 min 7 s long. The video is in Finnish; English subtitles are available on YouTube.

In short

I tested three of Chiang Mai's most interesting khao soi spots: Khao Soi Maesai stands for tradition, Khao Soi Mae Manee 2 for a wide choice of toppings and Khao-Sō-i for a modern, premium take. The winner is revealed in the video.

Places in the video

  1. 1

    Khao Soi Maesai

    A long-running family business in the Santitham area and a legendary tourist favourite. Expect to queue.

    Ratchaphuek Alley, Chang Phueak, Chiang Mai 50300

    Muscular Lair

    A popular gym in the Santitham area, where I trained between meals.

    Siri Wattana Market (Thanin Market)

    A traditional wet market and food hall, where I stocked the fridge with groceries and staples.

    193 Chang Phueak Road, Chang Phueak, Chiang Mai 50300

    Open in Google Maps In the video at 6:56

    Khao Soi Mae Manee 2

    Sister restaurant of the Michelin Bib Gourmand listed Khao Soi Mae Manee. It uses the same decades-old recipe and has more toppings, such as fish and organ meats.

    45/9 Lampang–Chiang Mai Superhighway, Chang Phueak, Chiang Mai 50300

    Open in Google Maps In the video at 8:16

    Warorot Market

    A busy market area in Chiang Mai's Chinatown. I bought pumpkin seeds here and visited the Chinese temple nearby.

    Wichayanon Road, Chang Moi, Chiang Mai 50300

    Khao-Sō-i

What is khao soi?

Khao soi is a noodle dish served in coconut curry broth, and one of Chiang Mai's best-known flavours.

How are the three restaurants different?

The difference is style: one stands for tradition, one for variety and one for a premium take.

When should you go for khao soi?

Go early. The most popular places get busy, especially at lunchtime. Opening hours vary, so check Google Maps before you go.

What can you do between meals?

I went to the gym and walked the markets. If you'd rather burn it off outdoors, read about two hikes in Chiang Mai: Monk's Trail and Doi Pui.

How do you fly from Finland to Chiang Mai?

Finnair flies nonstop from Helsinki to Bangkok, with a scheduled flight time of about 11 hours 15 minutes. From Bangkok you take a domestic flight to Chiang Mai, which typically takes about 1 hour 20 minutes. Chiang Mai airport is only 3 to 5 km from the Old City. You can get into town with the airport taxi desk, or with the Grab and Bolt apps.

Search flights from Helsinki (HEL) to Chiang Mai (CNX) on Google Flights. Prices and schedules change, so check them when you search.
